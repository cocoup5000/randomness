Privacy Policy — Jarvis (personal assistant)

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Jarvis is a personal, unlisted software project built and operated by a single individual (the developer) solely for their own personal use. It is not distributed, marketed, or made available to the public or to any other user.

Jarvis requests access to the developer's own Google Calendar (to view and create events) and Gmail account (to send email only — it cannot read, search, or otherwise access the inbox). This access is used exclusively to let the developer interact with their own calendar and send their own email through a personal chat assistant they built and run themselves.

Data obtained through these permissions is used only to fulfill the developer's own requests in real time and is not stored beyond what is needed to service that request, except for OAuth tokens, which are stored only on the developer's own private server in order to maintain authorization between sessions. No data obtained through this access is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and none of it is used for advertising or for training any model.

Because this is a single-user personal project, there is no account registration, no data collection from any other person, and no public service to speak of.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer directly.
